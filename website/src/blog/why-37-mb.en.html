Install four desktop mail apps and look at what they put on your disk. Three of them take between 361 and 850 MB. Regards takes 39. The difference is not clever compression. It is what is inside.

Most mail apps are a web browser with mail in it

A modern mail app has to display messages written in HTML, so it needs the engine of a web browser. The common answer is to ship one. Many desktop apps are built on a framework that bundles a complete copy of Chromium and a JavaScript runtime in every installer. Thunderbird brings the engine of Firefox, along with a calendar, chat, newsgroups and an add-on system.

That is where the hundreds of megabytes come from. It is also why, once such an app is running, you have a second browser open next to your browser.

Regards does not ship one

Your computer already has a web engine. Windows comes with one, so does macOS, and on Linux it is a library the system shares between apps. Regards draws its window with that engine and ships none of its own.

What is left is two programs written in Rust: the window, about 19 MB, and the mail engine, about 20 MB. Rust compiles to compact native code and needs no runtime alongside it. That is the whole installer.

It is not only the disk

A lighter app is lighter everywhere. One minute after launch, Regards holds 120 MB of memory. The other three hold between 299 and 470 MB.

That memory figure is the honest one: it counts every process Regards starts, including the system web engine it draws with. The engine is shared, but we count it as ours.

What you get out of it

  • A download that takes seconds, and updates that do too.
  • Room for everything else. A mail app stays open all day. It should not be the heaviest thing on your computer.
  • A faster start. Less to load is less to wait for: Regards shows your mail in 0.73 seconds.
  • Security updates from your system. The web engine is kept up to date by Windows, macOS or your Linux distribution, on their schedule, not ours.

Measured on 15 November 2026 on Windows 11 (AMD Ryzen 7 5800X, 32 GB of memory), each app with one account holding the same 20,000 messages: Regards 0.4.0 as published, Mailspring 1.26.2, Thunderbird 161.0 beta, Spark 3.32.4. Nobody was using the computer during the run, but two other mail apps were open in another user’s session. Thunderbird was measured in its beta version because that is where the test account is. Installed size is the app’s own folder: for Regards, 19.1 MB for the window, 19.9 MB for the mail engine and 0.1 MB for the uninstaller. The system web engine Regards uses is on the computer whether Regards is installed or not, so it is not in the 39 MB; it is counted in the memory. Sizes on macOS and Linux have not been measured yet.
